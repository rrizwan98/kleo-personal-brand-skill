## Trigger

A public Kleo tutorial by Jake Ward was matched to its published LinkedIn example. A separate writer used the skill with a reconstructed brief and historical evidence packet while the original final post was withheld. The first skill draft handled attribution carefully but read partly like a research explanation. The original's narrative momentum was stronger in the evaluator's qualitative assessment.

Sources: [official tutorial index](https://help.kleo.so/getting-started/chat), [worked video](https://www.loom.com/share/65d9a5adb2a74671ad318e822e539166), [reference post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jakezward_reddit-just-lost-82-of-its-ai-citations-activity-7381296490251444224-5kFd).

## Changes

Only version metadata in `SKILL.md` and two passages in `references/writing.md` changed.

1. For news-led LinkedIn posts, organize the copy around one reader-relevant implication and a small number of connected beats.
2. Keep essential attribution and uncertainty in public copy. Move calculation workings, source-access failures and repeated caveats into editorial notes unless they affect interpretation of the central claim.
3. Evaluate reading experience separately from factual completeness. A thoroughly checked draft can still need compression.
4. Use natural attribution in the post. Place supporting clickable references beside the draft or in notes when the publication destination does not render Markdown links. Keep URLs in the post when the brief or reader action requires them.

## Preserved behavior

Profile/product discovery, client memory, strategy, evidence gates, authorization, analytics and the workspace initializer are unchanged. Factual caveats must not be removed merely to make an opening more dramatic. There is no universal post-length requirement.

## Revision acceptance

| Check | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Central idea | A reader can identify one useful implication |
| Evidence | Important attribution and causal uncertainty survive compression |
| Public copy | No unnecessary narration of the assistant's research process |
| Source notes | Supporting references and audit details remain available outside the post |
| Voice | Missing authentic examples are still described as provisional |
| Scope | A requested short revision does not become a permanent style preference |

The recorded revised draft was 209 words after an explicit 180–230-word local revision request. That length was a test constraint, not a skill-wide rule. The original draft was retained. This was additional coaching and revision, not untouched first-pass performance.

## Interpretation and limits

This is one public-demo reconstruction, not a fresh paid Kleo run under identical conditions. The original private memory, full conversation and model settings were unavailable. The evaluator curated the research packet and was not blind to which system produced each draft. The writer was blind to the reference. No post was published and no reach, conversion or human-preference comparison occurred.

The case demonstrated a useful writing/revision workflow and exposed a readability gap. It did not prove 85% equivalence, exact personal voice, complete brand outcomes or general superiority. Output quality still needs review against the actual client's evidence and style.

## Verification scope

Revalidate package metadata and reference links, rerun the portable initializer tests for the public repository, and compare installed/source skill content before release. No new helper behavior or client-state schema change is introduced in this patch.
