Last Tuesday I asked to review every release note before it went out. Two notes then waited while I was in planning meetings.

I wanted to make sure the notes were useful. I had made myself part of every release instead, without explaining what I was checking.

So I wrote down two things: describe the change the user will see, and name any action they need to take.

Next week the team will draft against those checks. I will review the notes where there is unresolved customer risk.

I do not know yet whether that will make the process better. It gives us something clearer to try, and a way to discuss what still needs my attention.

For now, the mistake was small and specific. I asked people to wait for my judgment before I had made that judgment understandable.
