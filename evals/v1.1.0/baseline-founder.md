Automating a supplier follow-up starts with a responsibility question: who decides whether this email should go out?

Our product prepares the message and puts it in an approval queue. A designated person reviews it before sending. That leaves a useful place to check the context.

I would make three responsibilities explicit before setting up the workflow.

Who checks whether the supplier has already replied?

Who reviews the wording and the date being requested?

Who takes over when the reply needs a decision rather than another reminder?

Those responsibilities can belong to the same person. The point is to make them visible, so the team knows where the next decision sits.

Start with the draft, the reviewer and the handoff after the reply. Then decide which repetitive steps are worth automating. That gives you a process the team can explain before it becomes a process the team relies on.
