Two release notes waited while I was in planning meetings last Tuesday. I had asked to review every one before it went out.

That was a specific consequence of the rule I had chosen. There was work ready for a decision, and the decision was waiting for me.

I have now written down two checks: explain the change the user will see, and say whether they need to do anything.

Next week the team will draft against those checks. I will review notes with unresolved customer risk.

The experiment is small. We will see whether the written criteria are clear enough and where my attention is still needed.

I do not have a result to share yet. I have a clearer question: which part needs my judgment, and which part needs me to explain that judgment better?
