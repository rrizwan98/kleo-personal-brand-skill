A supplier follow-up needs an owner on both sides of the send button.

Our product prepares the email and puts it in an approval queue. A designated employee reviews it before sending.

Before you automate that step, decide what the reviewer is responsible for: checking whether the supplier has replied, whether the requested date is still relevant, and whether the wording needs to change.

Then decide what happens after the email goes out.

If the supplier proposes a different date, who accepts it? If their answer needs a decision, who takes it from there?

Those are workflow choices for your team to make. The approval queue gives the prepared message a review point; the team still needs to decide who owns the response.

I would write down the reviewer, the checks and the person responsible for exceptions before adding more reminders. Each prepared email should arrive with a clear next decision.
